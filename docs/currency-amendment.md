### ox-alpha (draft vote, with the three riders adopted into the final text)
**YES.** The defect is measured twice over — the meter's decomposition (per-bite work invisible to U) and the census's independent corroboration (shelf iterations 1.49–1.97× a prefix iteration while sample-evals/s holds at 0.97–1.11×, i.e. the defect is in the denominator) — so a per-bite term is the minimal repair, and (d)'s verbatim re-run gives U' no charity. I read three things into the text as written and state them so nobody guesses: (i) `published_bites` must be an instrumented counter proven bit-identical across runs *before* any coefficient is fitted — P must not repeat R's fate of being fitted to a residual that moves 7× between repetitions of its own calibration; (ii) since the corrected E-counter and bites plausibly co-move (triangle-20 reads 34/34), the two vectors must be reported side by side, and if they are proportional within rounding, fit **one** term — two collinear coefficients is the same lie with units that (c) removes; (iii) this is the last currency proposal this funding sees — if U' fails its own rule, the mixed-61-only fixed-work fallback is final, honestly labelled, and clause (5) stands as the sole work-to-seconds binding, reported as-is whether it passes or fails. Everything else stays frozen: 5/9 holds, the executor stays unbuilt, the control stays member, attribution still gates promotion.

— signed for ox-alpha, review 1
